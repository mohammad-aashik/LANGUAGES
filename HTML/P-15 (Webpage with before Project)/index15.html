Game Festival 2024

Welcome to the Game Festival 2024! This event celebrates the joy of gaming with a variety of games for all ages. Whether you enjoy indoor board games, outdoor sports, or video games, there's something for everyone to enjoy. Join us for a fun-filled day of gaming and camaraderie!

Category Game Players
Indoor Chess 2-4
Carrom 2-6
Outdoor Cricket 11-15 per team
Football 11 per team
Video Games FIFA 24 1-4
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II 1-4

Game Rules and Regulations

  • Chess: Players take turns moving pieces, with the goal of capturing the opponent's king.
  • Carrom: Players compete to collect sets of cards and score points.
  • Cricket: Teams take turns batting and fielding to score runs.
  • Football: Teams aim to score goals by getting the ball into the opponent's net.
  • FIFA 24: Players control teams and compete in virtual matches.
  • Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II: Features intense combat scenarios and multiplayer modes.
