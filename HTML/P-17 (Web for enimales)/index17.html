ANIMALES KINGDOM

The Animal Kingdom is a diverse and fascinating realm that encompasses a wide variety of creatures, each with its own unique characteristics and behaviors. From the majestic lions of the savannah to the colorful birds of the rainforest, the animal kingdom offers an incredible array of life forms that captivate our imagination and inspire awe. Whether it's the playful antics of dolphins in the ocean or the stealthy movements of predators in the wild, animals play a crucial role in maintaining the balance of ecosystems and contribute to the beauty and complexity of our natural world.

Terristrial Animales

Terristrial animales are those that live primarily on land. They have adapted to various terrestrial environments, from deserts to forests, and exhibit a wide range of behaviors and physical characteristics suited for life on land.

Aquatic Animales

Aquatic animales are those that live primarily in water. They have adapted to various aquatic environments, from oceans to rivers, and exhibit a wide range of behaviors and physical characteristics suited for life in water.

Amphibians

Amphibians are a class of animals that typically begin their lives in water and later transition to living on land. They have moist, permeable skin and often require humid environments to survive.
